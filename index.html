About Us

Sweet & Scientific is a student micro-enterprise by 10 Eduvos Tygervalley students for the module Bioentrepreneurship. We use South African indigenous plants to create healthy, calming desserts for stressed students.

Our Aim

We aim to establish a viable student-run micro-enterprise that provides affordable, functional snacks using South African indigenous plants while proving bioentrepreneurship viability and building practical business skills.

Objectives

  • Apply entrepreneurship principles
  • Demonstrate lab management skills
  • Achieve profitability
  • Promote sustainable use of indigenous biodiversity
